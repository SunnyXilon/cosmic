# Licensing and Sources

## Planet Texture Maps

Planet textures are downloaded from NASA/JPL Solar System Simulator texture maps:

https://maps.jpl.nasa.gov/tmaps/

NASA/JPL notes that some maps, especially gas giant maps, are representative because atmospheres change and spacecraft coverage varies.

## Planet Data

Planetary values are based on NASA Planetary Fact Sheet data:

https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/planetary/factsheet/

Data used in the app includes axial tilt, rotation period, orbital period, diameter, gravity, atmosphere, and mean temperature.

## Sun Data

Sun facts are based on NASA Science Sun facts:

https://science.nasa.gov/sun/facts/

The app uses these references for the Sun's star classification, diameter, visible surface, layers, core temperature, and fusion explanation.

## Uranus and Neptune Color

Uranus and Neptune visual treatment is informed by NASA Science coverage of their visible-color differences:

https://science.nasa.gov/solar-system/why-uranus-and-neptune-are-different-colors/

## Model Limitation

This is an educational model, not a scientific simulator. It intentionally compresses size, distance, and time so students can inspect the system on a classroom screen.

## Institutional Review

Before selling to schools, run a citation audit against the current NASA pages, verify all mission and moon notes with a science teacher, and keep this page packaged with the offline app so buyers can inspect data provenance.
