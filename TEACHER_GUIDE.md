# Teacher Guide

## Suggested Lesson Flow

1. Start on the Milky Way view and ask students to locate the solar marker.
2. Zoom to the Solar System and pause motion.
3. Open the Sun and discuss why it is a star, not a planet.
4. Turn labels and axes on.
5. Discuss which parts of the model use real NASA data: planet textures, axial tilt, rotation period, orbital period, diameter, gravity, atmosphere, and mean temperature.
6. Discuss which parts are compressed: planet size, distance, and time.
7. Open each planet detail page and use the overview, surface/atmosphere, classroom explanation, and exploration sections.
8. Use Compare to contrast Earth/Mars, Jupiter/Saturn, and Uranus/Neptune.
9. Switch to Student Mode for guided learning paths and vocabulary review.
10. Use Simulations for day/night, seasons, greenhouse effect, orbit speed, scale, rings, and moon systems.
11. Finish with the adaptive planet-specific Quiz tab.
12. Open Teacher Dashboard to review progress, weak topics, and export or print results.

## Level Selection

Choose Junior, Middle, or High before teaching. The selected level changes the wording across planet and Sun pages, lesson paths, simulations, quiz headings/questions, feedback, labels, and Why? explanations.

## Classroom Prompts

- Why can a true-scale solar system model not fit on a normal screen?
- Which planets spin retrograde?
- Why is Uranus shown as a pale blue-green planet with very few visible features?
- How do rocky planets differ from gas and ice giants?
- What evidence would you use to judge habitability?
- Why is the Sun classified as a star?
- What is the difference between surface, atmosphere, orbit, and rotation?

## Assessment Ideas

- Ask students to write one claim comparing two planets and support it with two data points.
- Ask students to explain one model limitation.
- Ask students to identify a planet from its texture, tilt, or data.
- Ask students to choose one planet and summarize its environment, exploration history, and one challenge for human missions.
- Use the Practice Coach to identify topics students missed and send them back to the related planet page.
- Assign a path or specific planet quiz from Teacher Dashboard.
- Print a worksheet from the selected lesson path.
- Export CSV for a class record, or print/save a PDF report from the browser print dialog.

## Progress and Motivation

- Wrong quiz answers now generate a simpler follow-up prompt and a direct route back to the related body page.
